Olethros Road 1 is a fictional road circuit included in the TORCS open source racing simulator. It is a mixed-layout track with 9 corners of varying speed and character, used as the primary test circuit for the AI-Enhanced F1 Simulator project.

The circuit has an expert reference lap time of approximately 175 seconds. The track combines slow technical corners with medium speed sections, requiring a balanced setup between mechanical grip and straight line stability.

CORNER CHARACTERISTICS

The circuit has 9 corners numbered T1 through T9, automatically detected from the expert steering trace. Each corner is classified by the error detection system based on the driver's telemetry compared to the expert baseline.

Slow corners (typically T1, T4, T7) require heavy braking from medium speed and precise throttle application on exit. These corners are the most common source of late braking errors and poor corner exit deficits. The driver must be patient with the throttle and wait until the car is pointing straight before applying full power.

Medium speed corners (typically T2, T5, T8) require trail braking on entry and smooth progressive throttle on exit. Understeer at the apex is a common issue caused by too much entry speed or turning in too early. The correct approach is a late turn-in point, hitting the apex cleanly, and using the full width of the track on exit.

Fast corners (typically T3, T6, T9) require commitment and smooth steering inputs. Any hesitation or sudden steering correction will unsettle the car and cause tyre slip. The driver should look through the corner and maintain a consistent steering angle through the apex.

RACING LINE

The optimal racing line on Olethros Road 1 follows the standard late apex principle for all corners. A late turn-in allows the driver to straighten the steering earlier on exit, applying full throttle sooner and maximising acceleration onto the following straight.

Track position offset from the centreline above 0.35 during cornering indicates the driver is running off the ideal racing line. Consistent deviation from the racing line through the same corner across multiple laps suggests a systematic error in turn-in point or apex targeting.

BRAKING ZONES

Each corner on Olethros Road 1 has a defined braking zone before the turn-in point. The expert baseline brakes approximately 100 to 200 metres before each corner depending on approach speed. Late braking by more than 25 metres compared to the expert baseline is flagged as a significant error.

Brake pressure should be applied firmly at the start of the braking zone and released progressively as the car slows. A sudden release of brake pressure can cause the rear of the car to step out. Trail braking, where a small amount of brake pressure is maintained into the corner, helps rotate the car towards the apex.

THROTTLE APPLICATION

Progressive throttle application is critical on Olethros Road 1. The exit of each corner leads into a section where acceleration time directly affects the lap time. Applying full throttle too early while still turning causes wheelspin and tyre degradation. The driver should wait until the steering is straightened before pinning the throttle.

Average throttle application below 0.75 on the straight sections between corners indicates the driver is lifting unnecessarily. Full throttle should be maintained on all straight sections to maximise straight line speed.

TYRE MANAGEMENT

Olethros Road 1 places moderate load on all four tyres due to the mix of braking, cornering and acceleration zones. Wheel spin above 0.2 on corner exits indicates the driver is over-throttling and causing rear tyre degradation. Smooth throttle application on exit is the primary method of preserving rear tyre life.

Track position deviation during cornering increases lateral tyre load and accelerates front tyre wear. Following the correct racing line reduces tyre stress and maintains consistent grip levels throughout the session.

SECTOR ANALYSIS

Sector 1 covers the first three corners. Time loss in Sector 1 is typically caused by a poor exit from T3, which reduces entry speed onto the straight leading into Sector 2.

Sector 2 covers corners T4 through T6 and is the most technical section of the circuit. Time loss in Sector 2 is the most common source of overall lap time deficit. The driver should focus on consistent braking points and clean apex hits through this section.

Sector 3 covers corners T7 through T9 and the final run to the finish line. A good exit from T9 is critical as it leads directly onto the main straight. Losing time in Sector 3 through a poor T9 exit costs speed across the entire following straight.
